# AI Agents

AI Agents are autonomous systems that can perceive their environment, reason about goals, and take actions to achieve them.
They often use Large Language Models (LLMs) like [[gpt-4]] or [[llama-3]] as their core reasoning engine.

Key features of AI agents:
1. Autonomy: Ability to operate without constant human intervention.
2. Reactivity: Responding to changes in the environment.
3. Proactivity: Taking initiative to reach goals.

Agents can be linked to other systems like [[Qdrant]] for memory or [[Web Search]] for real-time information.
